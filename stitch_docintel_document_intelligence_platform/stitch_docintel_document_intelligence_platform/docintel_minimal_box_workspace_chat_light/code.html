Under the stipulations of the submitted definitive transaction documents, indemnification liabilities and corresponding escrow retention are governed by two principal provisions:

1. General Indemnity Cap & Basket

The aggregate indemnification obligation for standard representations and warranties is strictly capped at $12,500,000 USD (representing approximately 10% of total enterprise valuation). A deductible basket of $650,000 applies prior to any recovery rights triggering.

2. Escrow & Survival Duration

Escrowed funds of $12.5M will be retained with JPMorgan Chase for a definitive survival window of 18 calendar months following the Effective Date. Unasserted remaining balances are scheduled for automatic release on day 548 post-closing.
